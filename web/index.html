Grammar Help

Present tense verbs

German verbs change based on who is doing the action.

Pronoun Ending Example
ich -e ich esse
du -st du isst
er/sie/es -t er isst
wir -en wir essen

Word Bank

  • sein — to be
  • haben — to have
  • gehen — to go
  • essen — to eat
  • trinken — to drink
  • hätte gern — would like
