Level 1 Lesson

Level 1 contains simple present-tense sentences to help you practice conjugating verbs according to the subject.

VERB CONJUGATION

A simple sentence consists of a subject and a verb. In German, verbs are conjugated according to who is doing the action.

  • I am drinking. - Ich trinke.
  • You are drinking. - Du trinkst.
  • He/She is drinking.- Er/Sie trinkt.
  • We are drinking. - Wir trinken.
  • They are drinking. - Sie trinken.

In general, verbs will have these endings according to the pronoun:

  • I, Ich: -e
  • You, Du: -st
  • He/She: Er/Sie: -t
  • We, Wir: -en
  • They, Sie: -en

For example:

  • I go. - Ich gehe.
  • You go. - Du gehst.
  • He/She goes. - Er/Sie geht.
  • We go. - Wir gehen.
  • They go. - Sie gehen.

Note: ‘She’ and ‘they’ are the same word: Sie. You can determine who is doing the action by understanding verb conjugations: -t vs. -en, sie trinkt vs. sie trinken.

Trick: Both ‘We’ and ‘they’ are plural pronouns and often have the same verb conjugation: -en.

Nouns follow the same verb rules as he, she, or they.

  • The man (he) is going. - Der Mann (er) geht.
  • The woman (she) is going. - Die Frau (sie) geht.
  • The dogs (they) are going. - Die Hunde (sie) gehen.

GENDER, 'THE'

Notice that nouns have gender, which affects which word is used for ‘the.’

  • Der Hund - The male dog
  • Die Hündin - The female dog
  • Die Hunde - The dogs.

Unfortunately, these are mostly memorized. This highlights the importance of speaking die Satzmaschine’s sentences out loud. We hope that with repetition, these articles (der, die, das) become automatic without you even thinking about them!

Trick: For now, you can think of plural nouns, such as, ‘the dogs,’ as always using ‘die’ for ‘the,’ regardless of the gender:

  • The men: Die Männer
  • The cats: Die Katzen
  • The students: Die Studenten

The plural form of nouns, unfortunately, must also be memorized. However, die Satzmaschine is here to help!

EXCEPTIONS

Die Satzmaschine has also loaded a small collection of adverbs, adjectives, and other nouns to help you produce more natural sentences.

Before using die Satzmaschine, please review the word bank and research any pronunciations if needed.

Here are the exceptions to the general rules. Don’t worry! These will come to you easily with repetition:

  • To be: bin, bist, ist, sind
    • Ich bin Student.
    • Du bist Student.
    • Er/Sie ist Student/Studentin.
    • Wir sind Studenten.
  • To eat: esse, isst, isst, essen
    • Ich esse.
    • Du isst.
    • Er/Sie isst.
    • Wir essen.
  • Would like to have: hätte gern, hättest gern, hätte gern, hätten gern
    • Ich hätte gern Kaffee.
    • Du hättest gern Kaffee.
    • Er/Sie hätte gern Kaffee.
    • Wir hätten gern Kaffee.
