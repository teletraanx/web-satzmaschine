About Die Satzmaschine

Das Problem und die Lösung | The Problem and the Solution

Many language resources focus heavily on vocabulary. While vocabulary is important, knowing words doesn't help much if constructing sentences requires you to stop and think:

  • "Do I use geht or gehen?"
  • "Does the verb go second?"
  • "Is it der, die, or das?"

Die Satzmaschine's goal is simple: repeat common sentence strutures until they become automatic.

Whenever I learned a new German word, I wanted to practie using it. However, when I tried to make a sentence, I wasn't just practicing that one word. I had to try to remember all the other words I knew, while also thinking about grammar, word order, verb conjugations, genders, articles, and sentence structure...

There is simply too much to think about at once.

Die Satzmaschine solves that problem by reducing the number of decisions you have to make. Instead of trying to invent a sentence from scratch, it generates one for you. Your job is simply to read it, understand it, and most importantly, say it out loud.

Just as we don't consciously think about English grammar, the goal is for German patterns to eventually feel just as natural, so you only have to worry about vocabulary. Because the vocabulary is limited within each level, your brain is free to focus on recognizing grammar patterns and building intuition for how German sentences are constructed.

Instead of memorizing grammar rules, you will repeatedly speak complete sentence aloud, and, over time, you will begin recognizing patterns instinctively. Your mouth will become faster than your brain.

Eventually, when you learn a new word, you will already know where to place it, how to conjugate it, etc.

Vokabular | Vocabulary

Vocabulary is still important, of course. Die Satzmaschine has selected words so every sentence is meaningful and useful. New vocabulary is introduced with the goal of emphasizing words you will encounter frequently, and each lesson includes a word bank for quick reference.

You may notice that die Satzmaschine does not display English translations. This is by design.

Once you've learned what a word means, the goal is to stop translating every sentence into English and, instead, begin understanding German on its own.

Every time you mentally convert "Ich trinke Wasser" into "I am drinking water," you're adding an extra step between reading and understanding.

Die Satzmaschine encourages you to build a direct connection between German words, grammar, and the ideas they represent. The included word bank is always available if you need a reminder, but during practice, the focus is on reading, speaking, and understanding German as German.

Die Satzmaschine isn't meant to replace other resources. Instead, it complements them by giving you a place to repeatedly practice the grammar and sentence structures that make new words useful.

The objective is not memorization.

The objective is intuition.

Generate a sentence.

Say it out loud.

Repeat.
